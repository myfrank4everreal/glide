Glide is an app that helps blind and low-vision people move around. It is built to
work entirely on your phone. This policy explains what it uses and what it keeps.

## What Glide does not do

- It does not send your camera images, location or any other data to us or anyone
  else. Glide's own code makes no network requests (see "Components from other
  companies" below).
- It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or crash reporting.
- It does not sell or share data, because it has none to share.

## Camera

Glide uses the camera to detect obstacles and to read text. Images are processed on
your phone and kept in memory only while they are processed. When you use Read text,
Glide takes one photo, reads the text in it, and deletes the photo straight away.

## Location

Glide uses your location only while the Navigate screen is open. It does not keep a
history of where you have been. If you choose **Save this spot**, the name you give it
and its coordinates are stored on your phone, encrypted by the phone's secure storage
(Android Keystore or iOS Keychain), until you delete the place or all your data.

## Motion sensors

The compass uses the phone's accelerometer and magnetometer while guiding. Readings
are not stored.

## Settings

Your settings (voice, vibration, language, contrast, whether you finished the
tutorial) are stored on your phone.

## Components from other companies

> To verify before publishing: these can contact their makers even though Glide's
> own code does not.

- **Text recognition** uses Google ML Kit, which runs on the phone. Google states
  that ML Kit may send anonymous usage and performance information to Google, and on
  Android the recognition model may be downloaded by Google Play services. Images
  are not sent.
- **Speech** is produced by your phone's own text-to-speech engine. Some engines
  offer online voices; you choose this in the phone's settings, not in Glide.

## Deleting your data

Settings → **Delete my data** erases your settings and saved places from the phone.
Uninstalling Glide also removes them. On iPhone, Keychain items can survive an
uninstall; using Delete my data first removes them for certain.

## Children

Glide is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

## Changes

If Glide ever needs to send data off the phone (for example, to look up street
routes), this policy will be updated first and the app will ask before doing so.
